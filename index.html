What electrical services does The Flash Electric provide?

The Flash Electric provides a wide range of residential and commercial electrical services in Athens, Watkinsville, and surrounding areas. Our services include electrical panel upgrades, lighting installation, wiring repairs, EV charger installation, generator installation, surge protection, and electrical inspections.

How much does an electrical panel upgrade cost?

The cost of an electrical panel upgrade depends on your existing electrical system, panel capacity, wiring condition, and installation requirements. The Flash Electric provides upfront pricing and clear options after assessing your electrical needs.

Do I need a permit for electrical work in Athens, GA?

Some electrical projects, including panel replacements, new circuits, and major installations, may require permits and inspections under local regulations. Requirements depend on the type of work, so contact our team to discuss your project before starting.

What do fuses and breakers do?

Both protect your wiring from too much current. A fuse melts and has to be replaced after it blows. A breaker trips and can be reset. If breakers trip often, call us: it is usually a sign something needs to be inspected. For after-hours problems, contact The Flash Electric and we will schedule service as quickly as possible.

How do I know if my electrical panel needs to be replaced?

Frequent breaker trips, flickering lights, unusual buzzing sounds, burning smells, or an outdated electrical panel can indicate a problem. Our licensed electricians can inspect your system and recommend whether a repair or panel upgrade is needed.

Can The Flash Electric install an EV charger at my home?

Yes! We provide electric vehicle charger installations, including Tesla charging solutions. Our electricians can evaluate your electrical panel, available capacity, and installation requirements to help you choose a suitable charging setup.

Does The Flash Electric provide free estimates?

Yes, The Flash Electric offers free, no-obligation in-home consultations and estimates. We explain the available solutions and provide clear, upfront pricing so you can make an informed decision without pressure.
